18. Sequential Clue Release

Implement an authoritative clue-release service.

For ordered Hunts:

1. The first authorized clue becomes available at start.
2. Completing the current stop unlocks the next stop and clue.
3. Future locked clue content remains unavailable.
4. The active Hunt query returns only authorized content.
5. Reopening the app restores the correct current clue.
6. Completion of the final required stop prepares Hunt completion.

Do not send all clue content to the client and merely hide it visually.
