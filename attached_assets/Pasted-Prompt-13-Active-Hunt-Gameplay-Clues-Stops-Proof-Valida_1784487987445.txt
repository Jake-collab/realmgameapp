# Primary Objective
Implement the Active Hunt journey:
```text
Ready Hunt
    ↓
Start Hunt
    ↓
Active Hunt Overview
    ↓
Current Authorized Clue
    ↓
Travel or perform activity
    ↓
Validate Stop Requirement
    ↓
Submit Required Proof
    ↓
Complete Stop
    ↓
Unlock Next Authorized Clue
    ↓
Complete All Required Stops
    ↓
Trusted Hunt Completion
    ↓
Completion Confirmation

The experience should feel:

* Immersive
* Focused
* Exploratory
* Clear
* Safe
* Modern
* Minimal
* Progress-oriented
* Game-like without appearing childish
* Suitable for solo and future group play

The current authorized clue or objective must remain the most visually important content.
